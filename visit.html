      Good to know

      Questions, answered

      How far ahead should I order?

      Bakes from the menu can usually be ordered for the same day. Custom cakes need at least 24 hours, and two-tier cakes 48 hours. For festivals, order a few days early.

      Can you make it eggless?

      Yes — anything marked “Eggless option” can be made without egg at the same price. Choose “Eggless” on the item, or switch it on in the cake builder. Look for the green dot for bakes that are always egg-free.

      What do the green, amber and red marks mean?

      Green dot: vegetarian, no egg. Amber dot: contains egg. Red triangle: non-vegetarian.

      How far do you deliver?

      Up to about 6 km from George Town as standard, further on request. Delivery charges are confirmed by the café when they confirm your order on WhatsApp.

      How should I store my bakes?

      Cakes, cheesecakes and tres leches: refrigerate and enjoy within 2–3 days. Cookies and millet bakes: airtight jar, up to 2 weeks. Savouries are best the same day — warm them for a few minutes before eating.

      How do I pay?

      Pay at pickup, on delivery, or by UPI once the café confirms your order. Custom cakes may need an advance.

      Can I change or cancel my order?

      Message the café on WhatsApp with your order code as early as possible. Changes are easy before your bake goes into the oven.
