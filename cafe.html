Straight out of a daydream.

Stone stairs wound in green, fairy lights at dusk, and a coffee that's never in a hurry — a garden café tucked away in Prayagraj.

The space

Come for the cake. Stay for the quiet.

A hand-painted sign promises coffee, cookies, dessert and savour. Behind it: climbing vines, a staircase that photographs better than it has any right to, and small tables where an afternoon disappears without anyone noticing.

Water comes in reusable glass bottles, the bakes come out of the oven in small batches, and nobody will rush you.
